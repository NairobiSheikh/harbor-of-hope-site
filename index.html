About us

Care that meets you where you are.

Harbor of Hope is a behavioral health and substance use disorder treatment program in Portland. We combine group therapy, one-on-one counseling, and psychiatric care so you can get the support you need without putting your life on hold.

Our mission

Harbor of Hope is committed to delivering compassionate, high-quality substance use disorder treatment that empowers individuals to achieve lasting recovery through individualized care, evidence-based practices, and community support.

A note from our leadership

Recovery is more than getting clean. It is getting your life back. Every person who walks through our doors carries a story, and many have carried it alone for far too long. We are here to walk beside you, not only through the hard first days, but through everything that comes after: finding a safe place to live, getting back to work, reconnecting with family, and building a life you are proud of. Whatever brought you here, you are welcome, and you are not alone. We believe in you, even on the days you don't believe in yourself.

The Harbor of Hope Leadership Team
Our programs

The right level of care for you.

After your assessment, our clinicians recommend the program that fits your needs. You can move between levels as you progress.

Partial Hospitalization (PHP)

Our most structured day program. Several hours of treatment most weekdays, then you return home each evening.

Intensive Outpatient (IOP)

Group and individual sessions a few days a week, designed to fit around work, school, and family.

Individual Counseling

One-on-one sessions with a clinician to work on recovery goals, coping skills, and relapse prevention.

Psychiatric Care

Psychiatric evaluation and medication management for co-occurring mental health conditions.

For my children

I called in the middle of the night, pregnant and scared, with no idea what to do. My two kids were staying with family, and I knew I had to get clean for them. Harbor of Hope answered that call.

I came in, and I kept coming, even after my baby was born. They help me bring her with me every morning. They set aside a private room so I could nurse her and let her nap while I went to my classes. Nobody ever made me feel like a burden.

They helped me find housing, and every test came back clean. Today I have over a year clean and sober, a beautiful apartment, and all of my kids are home with me. I am so grateful. They didn't just help me, they helped my whole family.

Elise Shepherd · Program graduate

From client to mentor

When I first walked in, I could barely walk. I was very sick, I had bad problems, and I knew that if I didn't stop using, I wasn't going to make it.

Harbor of Hope gave me resources, support, and people who believed I could change. I took in everything I could. Somewhere along the way, I started helping the others in my groups get their lives together too, and I realized that was what I wanted to do with my life.

I graduated, earned my CRM, and now I work here as a Certified Recovery Mentor. Every day I get to give back what was given to me.

Holly Walker · CRM, Harbor of Hope staff
